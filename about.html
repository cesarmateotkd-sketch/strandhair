About

Hair care, demystified.

What Strand Is

Strand is a free hair type quiz and natural hair care resource covering every texture from 1A to 4C. The quiz identifies your hair type and outputs a personalized product routine and tutorial guide built around your specific texture — not generic advice that treats all curly hair the same.

The blog covers the science behind natural hair care: why certain ingredients work on certain textures, how to diagnose problems (breakage vs. shedding, moisture vs. protein imbalance), and which products are actually worth buying at which price points. Every recommendation is based on ingredient research and formulation science, not brand partnerships or sponsored placements.

Our Approach

  • Evidence over anecdote — Where clinical research exists (rosemary oil vs. minoxidil, porosity and surfactant behavior, protein bonding in keratin), we cite it. Where it doesn’t, we say so.
  • No paid placements — Brands cannot pay to be featured. Product selections are based on ingredient quality, formulation, and community track record.
  • All textures — The quiz covers 1A through 4C with separate routines for each. Most natural hair content focuses on 4C; Strand covers the full spectrum.
  • Transparent monetization — Strand earns a commission on purchases made through Amazon affiliate links on this site. This does not change the editorial recommendations. See the full affiliate disclosure in our Privacy Policy.

Who Makes Strand

Strand is an independent site built and maintained by a single creator focused on filling the gap between generic hair care advice and the specific, science-backed guidance that different hair textures actually need. There is no team, no investors, and no editorial agenda beyond making useful content.

The hair type quiz, all 46 blog posts, and every product recommendation on this site were written from scratch. No AI-generated filler, no keyword-stuffed listicles written for search engines rather than people.
