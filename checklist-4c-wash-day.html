4C hair grows half an inch every month. Whether you see that growth depends entirely on your wash day. This checklist covers the full sequence — the right order, the right timing, and the steps most people skip that cause the most breakage.

01 Pre-Wash

  • Section hair into 4 parts with clipsKeeps detangling manageable and prevents re-tangling while you work
  • Apply pre-poo to dry hair: coconut oil, olive oil, or a thick conditionerCoats the strand before water exposure to reduce hygral fatigue
  • Cover with plastic cap — let sit 30 min minimum, or overnight

02 Detangle

  • Work one section at a time, all others clipped away
  • Start at the ends, work slowly toward the rootNever root-to-tip — this causes the worst breakage
  • Fingers first, then wide-tooth comb only — no bristle brushes
  • Apply detangler or conditioner for slip if needed

03 Cleanse

  • Rinse out pre-poo with warm water
  • Apply sulfate-free shampoo to scalp only — not the lengthShampoo rinses through the length on its own — scrubbing causes knots
  • Massage scalp gently with fingertip pads for 3–4 minutes
  • Rinse thoroughly until water runs clear
  • Low porosity: use a clarifying shampoo once per month to remove buildup

04 Deep Condition

  • Apply deep conditioner from mid-shaft to ends only — not the scalp
  • Cover with plastic cap
  • Sit under hooded dryer or heated cap for 20–30 minutesHeat is non-negotiable for low porosity — it opens the cuticle so moisture gets in
  • Rinse with cool water to seal the cuticle flat

05 Optional: ACV Rinse

  • Mix 1 part apple cider vinegar + 4 parts water
  • Pour over hair after rinsing conditioner, let sit 1 minute, rinse
  • Best for: high porosity hair — smooths raised cuticles, reduces frizzMax once per week. Skip if your hair feels dry afterward

06 Moisture: LOC or LCO

  • Apply to soaking wet hair — do not towel dry first
  • L — Liquid: water-based leave-in conditioner, section by section
  • O — Oil: penetrating oil (jojoba, argan, or olive) over the leave-in
  • C — Cream or butter: seals the moisture in
  • High porosity: LOC order  |  Low porosity: LCO orderLow porosity: cream before oil so the oil doesn't block absorption on flat cuticles

07 Style & Protect

  • Choose style while hair is still wet: twist-out, braid-out, or wash-and-go
  • Apply styling product section by section while soaking wet
  • Do not touch while drying — let the curl set undisturbed
  • Air dry or diffuse on low heat only
  • Overnight: pineapple or loose twists + satin bonnet or pillowcaseCotton absorbs moisture overnight and causes friction breakage — always use satin

Frequency Guide

Full wash dayEvery 1–2 weeks
Deep conditionEvery wash day
Protein treatmentEvery 6–8 weeks
Scalp massage3–4× per week
TrimEvery 8–12 weeks
Protective style max8 weeks in, 2 out

Signs your routine is working

  • Less breakage on wash day than last month
  • Hair stays moisturized 48–72 hours between wash days
  • Fewer single-strand knots at the ends
  • Visible length retention — measure on stretched hair, not shrunken
