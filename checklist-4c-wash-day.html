4C Wash Day Checklist hairtypequiz.hair

4C hair grows half an inch every month. Whether you retain that growth depends entirely on wash day. This checklist covers the full sequence in the right order — including the steps most people skip that cause the most breakage.

01 Pre-Wash
  • Section hair into 4 parts with clipsKeeps detangling manageable and prevents re-tangling while you work
  • Apply pre-poo to dry hair: coconut oil, olive oil, or a thick conditionerProtects the strand before water exposure — reduces hygral fatigue
  • Cover with a plastic cap and let sit 30 minutes minimum, or overnight
02 Detangle
  • Work one section at a time, all others clipped away
  • Start at the ends, work slowly toward the root — never root-to-tipRoot-to-tip detangling causes the most breakage of any single mistake
  • Use fingers first, then wide-tooth comb only. No bristle brushes on 4C
  • Add a detangler or conditioner for slip if hair is resistant
03 Cleanse
  • Rinse out pre-poo with warm water
  • Apply sulfate-free shampoo to scalp only — not the lengthLet it rinse through the length on its own — scrubbing causes tangles
  • Massage scalp gently with fingertip pads for 3–4 minutes
  • Rinse thoroughly until water runs clear
  • Low porosityUse a clarifying shampoo once a month to remove product buildup from flat cuticles
04 Deep Condition
Never skip
  • Apply deep conditioner from mid-shaft to ends only — not the scalp
  • Distribute with fingers or wide-tooth comb through each section
  • Cover with plastic cap. Sit under hooded dryer or heated cap for 20–30 minHeat opens the cuticle so moisture penetrates — critical for low porosity hair
  • Rinse with cool water to seal the cuticle flat
05 ACV Rinse
Optional
  • Mix 1 part apple cider vinegar + 4 parts water
  • Pour over hair after rinsing conditioner. Let sit 1 min, rinse cool
  • High porositySmooths raised cuticles, restores pH, reduces frizz — use max once per week
06 LOC or LCO Method
  • Apply to soaking wet hair — do not towel dry first
  • L — Liquid: water-based leave-in conditioner, section by section
  • O — Oil: penetrating oil (jojoba, argan, or olive) over the leave-in
  • C — Cream or butter: seals everything in
  • High porosityUse LOC order — oil between liquid and cream slows moisture escape
  • Low porosityUse LCO order — cream before oil prevents oil blocking absorption on flat cuticles
07 Style & Protect
  • Choose style while hair is still soaking wet: twist-out, braid-out, or wash-and-go
  • Apply styling product section by section to wet hair
  • Do not touch while drying — let the curl set undisturbed
  • Air dry or diffuse on low heat only
  • Overnight: pineapple or loose twists + satin bonnet or satin pillowcaseCotton absorbs moisture overnight and causes friction breakage every single night

Signs your routine is working
  • Less breakage on wash day compared to last month
  • Hair stays moisturized for 48–72 hours between wash days
  • Fewer single-strand knots at the ends
  • Visible length retention — measure on stretched hair, not your shrunken wash-and-go
