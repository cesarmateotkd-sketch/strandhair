4C WASH DAY CHECKLIST
=====================
The complete wash day sequence for 4C natural hair.
Use every 1–2 weeks for healthy length retention.

PRE-WASH (30 min before or overnight)
  [ ] Section hair into 4 parts and clip each section
  [ ] Apply pre-poo to dry hair: coconut oil, olive oil, or conditioner
  [ ] Cover with a plastic cap and let sit 30 min (or overnight)
  [ ] Pre-poo reduces hygral fatigue and protects strands during shampooing

DETANGLE (before wetting hair)
  [ ] Work one section at a time, keep remaining sections clipped
  [ ] Start at the ends, work slowly toward the root
  [ ] Use fingers first, then a wide-tooth comb only
  [ ] Add a detangler or conditioner for slip if needed
  [ ] Never detangle 4C hair dry — always with product and patience

CLEANSE
  [ ] Rinse out the pre-poo with warm water
  [ ] Apply sulfate-free shampoo to the scalp only — not the length
  [ ] Massage scalp gently with fingertip pads (not nails) for 3–4 min
  [ ] Let shampoo rinse through the length as it washes out — do not scrub
  [ ] Rinse thoroughly — product left on scalp causes buildup and itching
  [ ] Low porosity hair: clarify with a clarifying shampoo once a month

DEEP CONDITION (non-negotiable)
  [ ] Apply deep conditioner from mid-shaft to ends, not the scalp
  [ ] Distribute with fingers or wide-tooth comb through each section
  [ ] Cover with a plastic cap
  [ ] Sit under a hooded dryer or heated cap for 20–30 min
  [ ] Heat is critical for low porosity hair — it opens the cuticle for penetration
  [ ] Rinse with cool water to seal the cuticle

OPTIONAL: ACV RINSE (high porosity hair)
  [ ] Mix 1 part apple cider vinegar with 3–4 parts water
  [ ] Pour over hair after rinsing conditioner
  [ ] Let sit 1 min, rinse with cool water
  [ ] Smooths raised cuticles, reduces frizz, restores pH balance
  [ ] Use max once per week — overuse is drying

MOISTURE: LOC OR LCO METHOD
  [ ] Apply to soaking wet hair — do not towel dry first
  [ ] L — Liquid: water-based leave-in conditioner, section by section
  [ ] O — Oil: penetrating oil (jojoba, argan, olive) over the leave-in
  [ ] C — Cream or butter: seals everything in
  [ ] High porosity hair: LOC order (oil slows moisture escape through open cuticles)
  [ ] Low porosity hair: LCO order (cream before oil so oil doesn't block absorption)

STYLE
  [ ] Choose style while hair is still wet: twist-out, braid-out, wash-and-go
  [ ] Apply styling product section by section to wet hair
  [ ] Do not touch or scrunch while drying — let the style set
  [ ] Air dry or diffuse on low heat with no direct contact

OVERNIGHT
  [ ] Pineapple hair loosely at the crown with a soft scrunchie
  [ ] Or divide into 2–4 loose twists
  [ ] Cover with a satin bonnet or sleep on a satin pillowcase
  [ ] Do not use cotton — it absorbs moisture and causes overnight breakage

YOUR FREQUENCY GUIDE
  Full wash day:         Every 1–2 weeks
  Co-wash (optional):    Between wash days if scalp feels dry
  Deep condition:        Every single wash day — never skip
  Protein treatment:     Every 6–8 weeks (watch for stiffness = too much protein)
  Scalp massage:         3–4x per week, 4 min each time
  Trim:                  Every 8–12 weeks to prevent split end travel
  Protective style:      Max 8 weeks in, then 1–2 weeks out before reinstalling

SIGNS YOUR ROUTINE IS WORKING
  [ ] Less breakage on wash day
  [ ] Hair stays moisturized for 48–72 hours between wash days
  [ ] Less single-strand knots
  [ ] Visible length retention over 3–6 months
  [ ] Scalp feels clean, not tight or itchy between wash days


GROWTH TRACKING TIP
  Measure length on stretched hair (a braid-out or flat-ironed piece),
  not your shrunken wash-and-go. 4C hair shrinks 70-80% — you may be
  retaining more length than you realize.
