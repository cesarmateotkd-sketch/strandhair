Questions

What format is the guide?

A 12-page PDF you download right after buying. It reads well on a phone, and the checklist and tracker pages are made to print.

Will this make my hair grow faster?

No. Nothing has been shown to make healthy hair grow faster. The guide is about keeping the length you already grow by reducing breakage, which is why most Type 4 hair seems not to grow.

Is it only for 4C hair?

It’s written for 4A, 4B and 4C hair. Tight 3C curls share most of the same breakage risks, so much of it applies there too. Not sure of your type? Take the free quiz.
