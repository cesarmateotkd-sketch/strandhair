Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Strand (hairtypequiz.hair) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this site. Strand does not sell personal data to any third party.

Information We Collect

Analytics data

Strand uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) via Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use the site. GA4 collects:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • General geographic region (country/city level, not precise location)
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • Referring website or search query that brought you to the site

This data is aggregated and anonymous. Strand cannot identify individual visitors from analytics data. Analytics scripts are deferred until your first interaction with the page (mousedown, keydown, touchstart, or scroll) or after 30 seconds, whichever comes first.

Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising data

Strand uses Google Ads conversion tracking (AW-980643963) to measure traffic that originates from any Google advertising campaigns. If you arrived at this site from a Google ad, a conversion event may be recorded.

Strand also loads the Meta (Facebook) Pixel (ID: 1528517258881779) for audience measurement and campaign analytics. This pixel may set a cookie associated with your Facebook account if you are logged into Facebook. You can opt out of Facebook tracking via your Facebook ad preferences.

Email subscriptions

If you subscribe to receive the free hair care checklist or newsletter via the forms on this site, your name and email address are stored by Mailchimp (The Rocket Science Group, LLC). Strand uses this information only to send the requested content and occasional hair care updates. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.

Mailchimp’s privacy policy: mailchimp.com/legal/privacy

Information you do not provide

The hair type quiz runs entirely in your browser. Quiz responses are not transmitted to any server and are not stored by Strand.

Cookies

Strand does not set first-party cookies. Third-party cookies may be set by:

  • Google Analytics / Tag Manager — session and user identification for analytics
  • Google Ads — conversion tracking if you arrived from a Google ad
  • Meta Pixel — audience measurement if you are logged into Facebook

You can block or delete these cookies using your browser settings or a browser extension such as uBlock Origin. Blocking analytics cookies does not affect the functionality of this site.

Affiliate Disclosure

When you click an Amazon link on this site and make a qualifying purchase, Strand earns a small commission at no additional cost to you.

Affiliate commissions do not influence which products are recommended. Strand does not accept payment from brands to feature their products, and affiliate relationships are disclosed on every page that contains affiliate links.

Product prices and availability shown on this site are accurate as of the date of publication and may change at any time. Always verify the current price on Amazon before purchasing.

Third-Party Links

This site contains links to external websites, including Amazon product pages, Pinterest, and YouTube. Strand is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. This privacy policy applies only to hairtypequiz.hair.

Children’s Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. Strand does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through this site, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data Retention

Analytics data is retained by Google for 14 months by default. Email subscriber data is retained in Mailchimp until you unsubscribe, at which point it is deleted or archived in accordance with Mailchimp’s data practices.
